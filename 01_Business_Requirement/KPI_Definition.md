# KPI & Metric Definitions Matrix

This document defines the core performance indicators required for the Car Sales Dashboard.

## Category 1: Sales Overview
* **YTD Total Sales:** Cumulative sum of vehicle sales prices from January 1st of the current year up to the current date.
* **MTD Total Sales:** Cumulative sum of vehicle sales prices for the current calendar month.
* **YOY Growth in Total Sales:** Percentage change comparing current YTD sales against Previous Year-to-Date (PTYD) sales.
* **YTD vs PTYD Sales Difference:** Absolute currency variance between current YTD sales and PTYD sales.

## Category 2: Average Price Analysis
* **YTD Average Price:** Average sale price of vehicles sold during the current Year-to-Date period.
* **MTD Average Price:** Average sale price of vehicles sold during the current Month-to-Date period.
* **YOY Growth in Average Price:** Percentage growth of YTD average price compared to PTYD average price.
* **YTD vs PTYD Average Price Difference:** Absolute currency difference between YTD average price and PTYD average price.

## Category 3: Cars Sold Metrics
* **YTD Cars Sold:** Total count of vehicle units sold during the current Year-to-Date period[cite: 1].
* **MTD Cars Sold:** Total count of vehicle units sold during the current Month-to-Date period[cite: 1].
* **YOY Growth in Cars Sold:** Percentage variance in unit sales volume compared to the previous year.
* **YTD vs PTYD Cars Sold Difference:** Numerical difference in vehicle units sold between current YTD and PTYD[cite: 1].
